Privacy Policy for Agent4Creators

Effective date: September 10, 2026

LopezDev ("we," "us," or "our") operates Agent4Creators, a Telegram bot that helps creators configure AI assistants, share official links, and communicate with their audiences. This Privacy Policy explains what information the bot processes, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use Agent4Creators, we may process the following information:

  • Telegram user IDs and Telegram chat IDs.
  • Messages, commands, and information you voluntarily send to the bot.
  • Creator profile information, including display name, assistant name, writing preferences, disclosure text, content-mode settings, and official links.
  • Chat settings, including whether automated replies are enabled for a particular chat.
  • Operational information needed to provide, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the bot.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card information, government identification, private images, or other highly sensitive information to the bot.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate and provide Agent4Creators.
  • Connect a fan to the appropriate creator profile after the fan uses a creator share link.
  • Generate AI-assisted replies and provide creator-assistant functionality.
  • Store creator-approved links, profiles, and chat-level settings.
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and functionality of the bot.
  • Investigate technical issues, prevent misuse, and respond to support or deletion requests.

3. AI Processing

To generate responses, Agent4Creators may send message content and relevant creator configuration to OpenRouter and one or more AI model providers selected through OpenRouter. These providers process that information to generate a response to the user.

AI-generated responses may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for a particular situation. Agent4Creators is not a substitute for professional legal, medical, financial, mental-health, or emergency advice.

Unless clearly stated otherwise, the bot is an AI assistant and is not the creator personally.

4. When Information Is Shared

We may share information with service providers only when necessary to operate Agent4Creators, including:

  • Telegram, which provides the messaging platform.
  • OpenRouter and the AI model provider used to generate a requested response.
  • Hosting, infrastructure, database, logging, and security providers used to operate the bot.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the bot, provide creator settings and fan routing, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce applicable terms. Retention may vary depending on the information and how the bot is used.

6. Your Choices and Deletion Requests

You may request access to or deletion of information associated with your Telegram user ID or chat ID. Creators may request deletion of their profile, links, assistant configuration, and saved chat settings.

To make a request, email lopezdev@proton.me. To help us locate the relevant information, include your Telegram username and the nature of your request. We may need to verify that you control the applicable Telegram account before fulfilling a request.

7. California Privacy Notice

California residents may have privacy rights under applicable California law, including rights to request information about personal information collected, used, disclosed, or deleted, subject to legal exceptions and verification requirements. Agent4Creators does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

California residents may submit privacy requests by emailing lopezdev@proton.me.

8. Children and Age Restrictions

Agent4Creators is not intended for children under 13. Certain creator profiles or features may be intended only for adults aged 18 or older. Do not use adult-oriented features if you are under 18.

9. Third-Party Links

Creators may add links to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for the content, availability, security, or privacy practices of third parties. Review the privacy policy of every external site or service you visit.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will be posted at this page, and the effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.
